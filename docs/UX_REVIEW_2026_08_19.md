# Client UX review — 19 August 2026

Findings across `src/client/Controllers` and `src/client/UI`, at commit `22584ec`.
Ranked by player impact. Not independently re-verified since — check the
arithmetic before acting, though most of it is stated in the files.

## 1. Modal action buttons render outside the card

`ModalSystem.luau:153`, `:126`, `:91`. `actionsContainer` is parented to
`bodyContainer` at `UDim2.new(0, 0, 1, 16)` — scale 1 in Y. `bodyContainer` has
`AutomaticSize.Y` and so excludes a scale-positioned child from its own
measurement; the card never accounts for the buttons either.

With body height H: card bottom is `88 + H`, the button row runs `80 + H` to
`120 + H`. **32 of the row's 40 pixels sit below the card**, floating on the
backdrop. Three of four call sites hit it, including Create Civilian Character —
the first modal a new player ever sees. The `Height` path is wrong differently:
the buttons land mid-card with dead space beneath.

Parent `actionsContainer` to `card`, anchor `(0,1)`, or add the 56px to the
measured height.

## 2. Interface Scale only reaches UI that already existed

`SettingsController.luau:21`, `:112`. `_applyInterfaceScale` walks existing
descendants and is never hooked to `DescendantAdded`. Everything built lazily —
toasts, modal cards, tooltips, minimap captions, MDT rows, phone pages, dispatch
rows — never scales. Choosing **Large** gives a scaled shell around unscaled
content.

Two faults in the same line (`:38`, `clamp(..., 10, 36)`): the floor of 10
silently rewrites authored sizes, so an 8px `PhoneController` label at **Small**
becomes 10 — a 25% *increase* when the player asked for smaller; and the ceiling
of 36 shrinks the dashboard's 42px monogram at every setting.

## 3. InterfaceMotion does nothing for modals or toasts

`ModalSystem.luau:34` guards on `settings.GetSetting`, and **`SettingsController`
has no such method** — it exposes `Get()` and `Update()`. The guard is always
false, so motion stays on forever.

`NotificationSystem.luau:45` calls the right method, but it is index 1 of `ORDER`
in `init.client.luau:71` and is constructed before `SettingsController` is even
initialised, so `Get()` returns nil and nothing re-reads it.

Both should read `RoleplayOSInterfaceMotion` off their own ScreenGui at animation
time, the pattern `DeveloperDashboard.luau:1026` already uses. Five other
consumers read it correctly.

## 4. Toast body text truncates at about 48 characters

`NotificationSystem.luau:144`. `Size = UDim2.new(1, -44, 0, 16)` at `TextSize 12`
with `TextTruncate.AtEnd` in a toast fixed at 56 tall — 316px, roughly 48
characters. **Ten of the 24 hard-coded messages exceed it**, plus most server
`response.Message` text. The longest is 84 characters and loses the half
containing the instruction. Tapping dismisses, so the rest is unrecoverable.

Wrap the label, give it `AutomaticSize.Y`, drop the fixed 56.

## 5. Toasts ignore the orchestrator and land on two other controls

`NotificationSystem.luau:67`. Fixed `360` wide at `UDim2.new(1, -376, 0, 16)` —
the only client surface ignoring `UIOrchestrator` entirely. It completely covers
the Controls launcher (`ControlsController.luau:433`), and on a compact touch
phone a stack of toasts overlaps the emergency-lights panel. `DisplayOrder 100`
wins both.

## 6. MDT launcher is drawn on top of the radio panel

`MDTController.luau:1198` puts it at `x ∈ [18, 114], y ∈ [106, 144]`; the radio
body occupies `x ∈ [12, 312], y ∈ [12, 332]`. Entirely inside it, over the
content area, and MDT's `DisplayOrder 45` beats radio's 38. The radio hides only
when the MDT *panel* opens, not its launcher, so an on-duty officer on foot sees
this constantly.

Also a double inset: the ScreenGui is mounted `CoreUISafeInsets`, so adding
`layout.NativeTop` applies it twice and pushes the launcher 48px low.
`CustodyController.luau:1095` and `RadioController.luau:588` both document having
fixed exactly this; MDT is the one still carrying it.

## 7. Minimap waypoint distance is clipped away

`MinimapController.luau:277`. The viewport is 168px wide with
`ClipsDescendants`. The distance label sits at `marker.x + 82` to `+118`, so
**for any waypoint right of about 30% of the map width the `"%dm"` readout is
entirely clipped** — the most useful number on the panel, disappearing based on
where the destination happens to be. POI captions clip mid-glyph the same way,
with no ellipsis.

## 8. Tooltips are mouse-only

`TooltipSystem.luau:103` connects only `MouseEnter`/`MouseLeave`, neither of
which fires on touch. Every place the design leans on a tooltip to recover
truncated text is unrecoverable on phone and tablet — gamepass name and
description, role choice, minimap captions, progression role names.

`Attach` also has no dedup, never disconnects, and is called *inside* the layout
binding at `DeveloperDashboard.luau:3405` and `:3454`, which republishes per
frame while a window is dragged. Currently masked because both elements are
hidden, but it is still a leak.

## 9. Needs bars: white text at 2:1 contrast in a 10px pill

`NeedsController.luau:8`, `:161`. White label over the fill measures **2.01:1**
on FOCUS and **2.75:1** on STAMINA — both fail AA, and FOCUS fails 3:1 too.
Contrast also changes as the bar drains, so legibility depends on the value being
displayed. The pill is 10px tall holding 9px type that `SettingsController`
bumps to 10, then `HudScale 0.72` on a large phone makes it a 7.2px pill. It also
sits with zero clearance above the hotbar reserve, where the speedometer adds 10.

## 10. The phone is resized instead of scaled on desktop

`PhoneController.luau:331`. The compact branch correctly holds `body.Size` at the
326×610 design and scales; the desktop branch sets `bodyScale.Scale = 1` and
shrinks the frame while every internal is authored in fixed pixels. On a
1440×900 laptop the content area drops 28% while the 24px heading and 92px
island stay put. At the floor they occupy 46% of the width. This is the specific
mechanism behind "cramped", and the fix is written twelve lines above it.

## 11. Design tokens exist and almost nothing uses them

Only **4 of 45** client files import `DesignTokens`. The two surfaces that sit on
top of everything else — modal and toast — use a different palette entirely:
accent `#5B9CFF` against the token `#1B89BF` is a different hue, not a shade. A
dialog opened from the MDT is visibly a different application from the MDT.

**20 distinct corner radii** in use where the system defines four. Title sizes
range 17–24 against a token of 18.

Migrate `ModalSystem` and `NotificationSystem` first: they overlay every other
panel, so they carry the most cross-surface comparisons for the least code.

## Suspected

- `DeveloperDashboard.luau:3336` hard-codes `local horizontal = true`, making ~42
  lines of sidebar layout unreachable while still doing its work every publish.
- Speedometer clears a compact phone's top band by only 4px.
- Three vehicle ScreenGuis share `DisplayOrder = 12`.
- Phone signal/wifi/battery glyphs are decoration shaped like a readout.
- `VehicleSpawnerController` never binds the orchestrator.
- `menuToggle` is ~36px on touch, under the 44pt minimum — possibly a knowing
  trade to match Roblox's own controls.

## Checked and clear

Every value the speedometer displays has a writer, and unknown cases render `--`
rather than a fake number. The eight zero-writer attributes are all
Studio-authored on workspace instances, which is correct. `CharacterCreator`'s
layout binding is the cleanest responsive code in the client.
